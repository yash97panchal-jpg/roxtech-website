Privacy notice

How RoxTech handles information from website visitors, inquiries and consultation bookings.

Information you provide

When you request a quote, book a consultation, or email RoxTech, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, contact details, organization, and project description. Please leave passwords, payment information, and other sensitive details out of website forms.

How we use it

We use this information to reply to inquiries, arrange consultations, prepare estimates, and operate and secure the website. Inquiry and booking records are kept in our Cloudflare-hosted database and are accessible to authorized RoxTech administrators. If you email us, our email service handles message delivery.

Cookies and website analytics

The restricted admin area uses session cookies to protect access. Google processes sign-in information when an administrator uses Google sign-in. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure site performance without visitor tracking cookies.

Retention and privacy requests

Retention depends on the inquiry, ongoing work, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. To ask about the information we hold, correct your details, or request deletion, email hello@roxtech.online.
