Solving production bottlenecks in manufacturing

A precision manufacturer with three production plants managed assembly schedules and material intake across separate spreadsheets. Inventory counts were frequently out of sync, delaying customer dispatches by several days.

We built a unified ERP and shop-floor tracking system that connects raw inventory, assembly lines and warehouse dispatch into one live database.

  • Barcode scanning at each work station for instant job tracking
  • Automated raw material reorder alerts based on production pace
  • Live throughput dashboards for plant supervisors and managers
  • Full batch traceability from intake to final customer shipment
