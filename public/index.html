Common questions

Straightforward answers about our engineering process, pricing, and handover.

How long does a project take?

Most web portals and mobile applications take 8 to 12 weeks. Larger ERP rollouts typically take 3 to 6 months. We ship working increments every two weeks, so you test features regularly before launch.

How does pricing work?

We work on fixed-price milestones with a clearly documented scope, or dedicated monthly engineering pods for ongoing work. There are no hidden fees, and any scope changes require written approval first.

Who owns the code?

You own 100% of the code, intellectual property, architecture files, and data from day one. When a milestone completes, full repository access and documentation transfer directly to your team.

What support do you offer after launch?

Every build includes 30 days of free post-launch support and bug fixes. Afterward, we offer monthly maintenance plans covering 24/7 uptime monitoring, security patches, backup management, and ongoing feature updates.

Which technologies do you use?

We build with modern, proven stacks: React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, Django, PostgreSQL, and Flutter for cross-platform mobile apps. For cloud infrastructure, we deploy to AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

How do you handle security?

Security is built into every release. We follow OWASP standards, enforce encrypted data storage, configure role-based access control, and perform automated vulnerability scans before any code reaches production.
